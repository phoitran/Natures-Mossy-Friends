## Visual review

All three selected outputs were visually inspected. Snow is visibly granular, hand-packed and slightly uneven. Snowmen have broad bases, short twig arms and simple natural features. The small cottages are solid snow mounds with supported twig roof slopes, a light snow dusting, shallow door impressions and stones only at ground level. An initial village draft was revised specifically to remove stones from walls. Animal bodies stay low and joined to the ground; the mouse uses fallen leaves for ears, the rabbit has short broad snow ears, and the turtle has broad low flippers. No image contains hollow/enterable shelters, commercial props, people, plastic eyes, text or watermarks.
