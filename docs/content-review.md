# Natures Mossy Friends content status

The catalogue contains 15 active draft activities and 20 archived guides, with 175 deterministic SVG step panels. They have not been physically trialled or professionally reviewed. Ages, material quantities and active times are provisional starting points. Do not present generated finished images as photographs of tested projects.

## Editorial choices

- Every recipe specifies its base, joining method, quantities, adult tasks and cleanup; no unspecified “reviewed method” placeholders remain.
- Shared plans use separate arranging and construction jobs. Seasonal eligibility follows the replacement activity briefs and the complexity of each craft.
- Woodland villages use a low box-supported lean-to; boats are five-twig rafts tested in a shallow tray; stars use five equal twigs with five outer joins.
- Pinecone snowy forests use washable white poster paint, matching the image brief. Painted cones remain indoor craft materials and must not be returned to habitats. This differs from the PRD’s original removable-paper-snow concept and needs owner/editor review.
- Bookmark pressing is a separate 7–14-day-or-longer waiting stage. Paint drying follows the chosen product label. Active making times do not include those waits.
- Diagrams are code-authored vectors that describe the actual recipe actions. Generated result galleries are inspiration, not evidence of successful physical construction.

## Before practical release

Have an experienced adult activity facilitator trial every recipe, the eligible age variations, stability and joins, plant/soil handling assumptions, tool supervision, timings, cleanup, and the correspondence between generated examples and actual outcomes. Replace or supplement at least one generated example per activity with an original photo of a completed trial. Check SVG labels visually on a phone and with a screen reader.

## Spring collection replacement — November 22, 2026

The active spring collection is exactly Meet the mud creatures (both groups), Dandelion Crown (Young Creators), Muddy Faces (both groups), and Spring Fairy Garden (Young Creators). Five prior spring IDs are archived, not repurposed. New IDs SPR-06–09 preserve old project/photo/progress associations. Each replacement has a description, quantified materials, five illustrated steps, three finished examples and its own captioned music-backed guide.

The user called the flower activity “Daphodil Crown,” but supplied photographs and the 81.6-second screen recording show dandelions. The guide is therefore named Dandelion Crown. The original video was reviewed for the under-cross, upward wrap and rightward tail sequence and the overlapping stem-loop join. It was not published or reused in the site. The original tutorial diagrams and instructional wording describe the same technique.

Mud recipes use clean clay-rich soil, gradual water addition and a flat alternative when the soil will not hold. Mud faces are temporary, permission-based and gently removed without scraping bark. Spring shelters visibly lean against a tree; loose bark or leaves rest on the twig frame. The ground is not dug into, and living moss or bark is not harvested. Stable rocks are an alternative support, with adult help for low roof stones.

### Sources checked for the crown distinction and technique

- User-supplied images 9–11 and screen recording, inspected locally.
- [RHS: potentially harmful garden plants](https://www.rhs.org.uk/prevention-protection/potentially-harmful-garden-plants) identifies Narcissus/daffodil as harmful if eaten and a skin irritant; the guide explicitly avoids substituting daffodils.
- [Red Ted Art: flower crowns](https://www.redtedart.com/how-to-make-flower-crowns/) corroborates the wrapping motion shown in the recording. Wording and diagrams are original.

The crown is not a slit-stem chain or three-strand braid. Its close-ups show one flower added at a time. Generated crowns are illustrative examples, not proof of the precise join or physical performance.

## Summer collection replacement — November 22, 2026

The active summer collection is exactly A summer nature mandala, Rocky friends, Our wilderness creatures and Sandy Explorer. All except Our wilderness creatures support both age groups and shared making. Wilderness creatures is an older-maker guide. The five earlier summer IDs remain archived so saved project associations are preserved. New IDs are SUM-06–09.

The 19 supplied references inform materials, construction and style; screenshots are not reused in public assets. Sand examples are deliberately small and child-made, with uneven low walls, shells, pebbles, optional flowers and a clearly open route. Water is optional and may soak into the sand quickly. The instructions do not promise permanent water-filled channels. Beach examples stay away from waves and moving water, and channels are filled after play.

Rocky friends are flat loose compositions; their basic directions do not rely on glue, heavy rocks or balancing upright statues. Wilderness creatures use a flat bark/leaf/stone body with tucked twig limbs, layered leaves and optional already-detached moss. Living moss and bark are not stripped; loose grass or leaves are alternatives. Children are encouraged to invent their own creatures rather than reproduce polished examples exactly.

## Winter replacement guides

Make a little snow man offers a low two- or three-ball build for both age groups and shared making. A pocket-sized snow village and Snowy Friends are for older makers. Village models are hand-sized solid snow mounds with twig roofs resting on snowy slopes, shallow door imprints and ground-level stones. Snow animals use low bodies, broad joins and short details; the examples are a mouse, rabbit and turtle. All three guides explain suitable packing snow, warm-up breaks, approved locations, collection of every decoration and the temporary nature of the craft. The five earlier winter guides remain available for existing saved projects.

## Winter reference-focused revision

The three winter guides retain their IDs and five-step structure. The snowman guide includes the pine-cone hat, carrot nose and upside-down boots variation. Village construction now includes rounded sprig-topped houses and low snow-block forms alongside the twig-roof cottage. Snowy Friends expands to six reference-based scenes, with mice, rabbit pairs, two cat designs, a bear and a turtle. The instructions permit taller ears and seated animal forms with grown-up help, using broad joins. These remain draft guides; images illustrate variations rather than proving practical testing.
